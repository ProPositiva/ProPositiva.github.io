Seamless Experience

Most outdoor spaces are an afterthought — a scattered collection of furniture, a gas grill pushed against a wall, uneven tiles that shift with every season. They promise relaxation and deliver frustration.

IRONDECK is different. Built around industrial-grade aluminum, engineered drainage, and a philosophy that every component should speak to every other, IRONDECK transforms any outdoor space — garden, terrace, rooftop — into a seamless environment where you want to live.

Feel the solidity underfoot. Smell the evening air from under a perfectly sheltered pergola. Cook with the confidence of a professional kitchen, steps from your living room door.

The Three Systems

BUILT AS ONE

Three systems, designed together, that work as one.

System 01 / Foundation

STRATA

Modular Deck & Outdoor Flooring System

That first step outside — solid, level, permanent. The ground that holds everything together.

STRATA provides the structural base for every outdoor environment. Engineered to sit over soil, concrete, or uneven terrain, it resolves the drainage, leveling, and long-term durability issues that plague conventional outdoor flooring. Every surface feels intentional, every edge aligned.

Installs over any terrain — soil, concrete, slopes
Built-in drainage channels prevent pooling
Climate-resistant materials rated for extreme weather
Modular grid locks with KUBIK and AXIS systems
Learn More About STRATA
System 02 / The Kitchen

KUBIK

Modular Outdoor Kitchen System

The sound of searing. The scent of smoke and evening air. A kitchen that rises to meet you.

KUBIK is the functional heart of IRONDECK. Industrial aluminum frames, engineered to the same tolerances as commercial kitchen equipment, now live in your outdoor space. Integrate storage, appliances, and work surfaces that are built for real cooking — not just weekends, but every night you choose to be outside.

Industrial aluminum frame — weatherproof, maintenance-free
Appliance-ready modules with integrated connections
Configurable countertops, storage, and sink modules
Expandable — add modules as your lifestyle grows
Learn More About KUBIK
System 03 / The Sky

AXIS

Modular Pergola & Roofing System

Shelter without walls. Light filtered through precision. A sky that's finally on your terms.

AXIS brings the structure overhead, completing the outdoor room. Engineered to connect directly to STRATA and KUBIK, AXIS provides shade in summer, rain protection through the seasons, and a framework ready for louvered panels, integrated lighting, and solar upgrades. It is the defining gesture of the space — the moment your backyard becomes a destination.

Direct integration with STRATA deck system
Ready for louvers, screens, and solar panels
Integrated lighting channel along every beam
Rain-rated roofing options available
